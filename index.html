Garrett Motion · data-centre backup generators

How much of the data-centre generator turbo market does Garrett supply?

Data centres need an air permit for their backup generators. Permits say which engines are installed; the engine data sheets attached to many applications name the turbocharger part. Putting the two together estimates Garrett's share from public documents, and every number on this page links back to a source.
